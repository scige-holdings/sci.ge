SCI.GE — South Caucasus Science & Innovations

SCI.GE is a flagship 3-letter digital asset dedicated to science, artificial intelligence, biotechnology, and research initiatives in Georgia and the South Caucasus.

Originally reserved for an independent scientific and technological initiative, the SCI.GE domain is currently open to strategic partnership or direct acquisition inquiries from qualified institutions, research centers, laboratories, and technology companies through December 20, 2026.
